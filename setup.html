One MENEI account. Your cloud sync included.

Open Settings → MENEI account & cloud sync. Sign in, or create your account and confirm your email if prompted. You do not need a separate cloud, ChatGPT or OpenAI account.

Complete iPhone & Android installation and setup guide →

Reminders

  1. Save your workout start time and time zone in Settings → Workout time & reminders.
  2. Enable push on each device and allow notifications. iPhone/iPad requires the Home-screen web app on a supported system.
  3. Test device display, then Test with app closed · 2 min. Normal workout reminders arrive about one hour before the saved start.

Your data

Settings → Your data has Export backup, Import / merge backup and Saved device snapshots. Keep existing records and backups when troubleshooting. Do not clear site storage or uninstall to force an update.
