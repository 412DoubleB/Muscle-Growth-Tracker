## Ready-to-copy invitation

Try MENEI for training, nutrition and progress:
https://412doubleb.github.io/Muscle-Growth-Tracker/start.html

Open the guide in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. It shows how to add the
MENEI icon to your Home screen, create your MENEI account, and set up your gym,
program, nutrition, units and workout reminders. Your MENEI account includes
cloud sync; no ChatGPT account, separate cloud account or AI download is needed.

For reminders, allow notifications on your phone and save your workout start
time. iPhone users must open the installed Home-screen app to enable push.
The guide has separate Apple and Android steps and a closed-app notification test.

AI gym research is a limited beta: first 20 accounts, five attempts per account.
Review the equipment suggestions and correct your gym checklist before saving.

9. Android permission, site category, sounds/pop-ups (`#android-reminders`).

11. Open-app rest sounds/mute and media volume (`#audio`).
12. Optional calendar file import, not a live subscription (`#calendar`).
13. Export/merge backup and non-destructive updates (`#backup`).
14. Email, old-version, AI and two-device troubleshooting (`#help`).

No normal tester needs Supabase configuration, cloud URLs, VAPID values, provider
API keys, separate AI accounts, wearable authorization or a server setup screen.
Those are operator tasks. Preserve first-use context: existing private-preview
and live accounts/origins are separate until deliberately reconciled; do not
promise history transfer simply because an email address is the same.

The guide uses official Apple/WebKit and Chrome installation/notification
references, checked October 23, 2026. Device menus may vary. iOS/iPadOS Web Push
requires a Home-screen web app on 16.4+ with the required APIs. Rest sounds and
closed-app push have different audio/permission behavior. No delivery/sound
guarantee, auto-enabled permissions or App Store/Play Store listing is claimed.
