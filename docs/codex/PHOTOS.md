## Locked production standard
Only when future image work is authorized:
1. One exercise at a time. START only first; no FINISH until the owner approves START. Do not advance until START and FINISH pass QC.
2. One medium-tone athletic male; short dark hair/trimmed beard; shirtless, black shorts and plain white shoes. Charcoal wall/dark rubber floor; only required equipment.
3. No embedded title, text, labels, arrows, diagrams, borders or combined poses. App UI separately renders equipment, muscles, setup, cues and mistakes.
4. Match the approved START model, camera, equipment, proportions, lighting and setting in FINISH. Biomechanics and exact equipment take priority.
5. Do not change approved equipment or format midstream. Retain distinct variants. Wrist curl and reverse wrist curl require separate START/FINISH pairs, with the forearm supported through the wrist.
6. Do not crop composites into supposedly approved single poses or invent standalone muscle-highlight assets. Older male/female/profile-tone ideas remain historical context; they do not authorize rewriting the locked current production style.

## Reconciliation method
Use the original chat's exercise/pose, user approval message, attached/generated file identity and SHA-256 together. Record rejected/superseded candidates without deleting them. Record a verified status only when the evidence binds the approval to the exact image. A proposed match based on creation time stays a candidate. Never force the owner to approve the entire library again because retrieval is incomplete.
