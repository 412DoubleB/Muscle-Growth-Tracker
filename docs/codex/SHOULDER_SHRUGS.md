# Shoulder shrugs

The owner requested shoulder shrugs in the library and automatic shoulder-day
programming. This candidate adds Dumbbell Shrug and Barbell Shrug with independent
guides, upper-trapezius anatomy, exact equipment requirements and separate histories.
All 240 existing catalog records remain byte-equivalent. No photos are generated,
borrowed or rebound; the new variants retain an explicit missing-media status.

## Programming

- Newly generated The MENEI Split shoulder/abs days retain eight exercises:
  five deltoid slots, one shrug accessory and two core slots.
- Newly generated Bro Split shoulder days retain six exercises: five deltoid
  slots and one shrug accessory. The shrug replaces the former generic sixth
  shoulder slot rather than extending session volume.
- New original-program continuation shoulder days use their final generic
  shoulder slot for the same accessory. The original 28-day source is unchanged.
- Choose one eligible shrug variation for the actual gym, respecting exclusions
  and the existing rotation. If neither is suitable, another compatible shoulder
  exercise fills that slot. Supported smart-plan settings continue to use their
  existing movement-eligibility rules; age alone never restricts an exercise.
- Full Body, Upper/Lower, PPL and seven-group generic shoulder slots retain
  deltoid work. Shrugs cannot replace their only shoulder slot.

Dumbbells are logged per hand. Barbell loads are logged once as the combined
external load; the existing two-sided plate rules still apply. Both use the
existing checked-set reps/RIR and historical progressive-overload engine, gym
increments and limits. The guides emphasize controlled shoulder elevation,
neutral neck and long arms, without shoulder rolling or knee-driven bouncing.

There is no migration or automatic rewrite of saved plans, routines or logs.
Newly generated shoulder days incorporate the accessory. Existing workouts can
use the normal Add exercise or Swap controls; recorded entries remain protected.
Other saved settings, account boundaries, original backups, nutrition, reminders,
artwork and the mascot are unchanged.

## Validation and publication

The focused shrug suite covers original-record hashes, equipment/avoidance
fallback, shoulder/core coverage, age/ability choices, continuation, recorded
history, dumbbell per-hand limits and paired barbell plate increments. Full
regression/static checks and the final candidate receipt are retained outside
the public source tree. A private sample preview is separate from the live app.
This change is prepared for review; live publication needs explicit owner approval.
